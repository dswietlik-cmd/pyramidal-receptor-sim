# Results draft — final n=20 experiment

## Baseline matching
The healthy state fired at 10.96 ± 0.91 Hz. The input-driven and NMDA-driven pathological states were closely matched for baseline hyperexcitability at 24.61 ± 1.09 Hz and 23.61 ± 5.62 Hz, respectively (n=20 paired seeds per state).

## State-dependent pharmacodynamic response
**Perampanel.** Healthy functional EC50 12.70 nM (bootstrap 95% CI 11.37–13.99); Input-driven EC50 not estimable within the tested concentration range (maximum observed suppression 17.6%); NMDA-driven functional EC50 16.07 nM (bootstrap 95% CI 12.72–17.83).

**Memantine.** Healthy functional EC50 175.47 nM (bootstrap 95% CI 168.94–181.56); Input-driven EC50 not estimable within the tested concentration range (maximum observed suppression 29.9%); NMDA-driven EC50 not estimable within the tested concentration range (maximum observed suppression 27.1%).

**Diazepam.** Healthy functional EC50 8.12 nM (bootstrap 95% CI 7.36–8.85); Input-driven EC50 not estimable within the tested concentration range (maximum observed suppression 6.7%); NMDA-driven functional EC50 8.63 nM (bootstrap 95% CI 7.86–9.74).

## Healthy-preserving window
For input-driven pathology, the largest mean pathological suppression observed on the tested grid while preserving at least 80% of mean healthy firing was:
- Perampanel: 7.6% suppression at 8 nM, with 82.6% healthy firing preserved.
- Memantine: 2.5% suppression at 50 nM, with 92.8% healthy firing preserved.
- Diazepam: 1.4% suppression at 5 nM, with 92.3% healthy firing preserved.

For nmda-driven pathology, the largest mean pathological suppression observed on the tested grid while preserving at least 80% of mean healthy firing was:
- Perampanel: 11.3% suppression at 8 nM, with 82.6% healthy firing preserved.
- Memantine: 6.9% suppression at 50 nM, with 92.8% healthy firing preserved.
- Diazepam: 0.6% suppression at 5 nM, with 92.3% healthy firing preserved.

These functional EC50 values are properties of the calibrated drug–receptor–neuron system and should not be interpreted as molecular binding constants or clinical exposure targets. The healthy-preserving analysis is restricted to the tested concentration grids and is therefore descriptive rather than a clinical therapeutic-index estimate.
